Data & methods

Every signal has a source.

Inspect the observations

One panel row is one observed value for one indicator, geography and date. We retain each source’s daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly or annual frequency. Missing and nonnumeric rows are omitted; duplicate dates are not allowed. We do not invent daily readings between economic releases, splice investment histories, or include stock prices.

Globe geography is independent of economic coverage. Country and territory outlines come from public-domain Natural Earth maps, checked against the UN country and area roster. Gray places remain identifiable even without economic data. Where several geographic areas share one economic series, the cards name the shared aggregate. Boundaries follow the mapping source and do not express a position on sovereignty. Tiny islands are preserved at the source map's resolution, not represented as survey-grade coastlines.
