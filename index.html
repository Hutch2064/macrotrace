The readout

Growth has held up, but the price of resilience is visible.

Labor, inflation, growth, rates, housing, energy, credit, and public markets rarely move in isolation. This report standardizes them into comparable windows and asks a practical question: where is the current economy strong, where is it strained, and what has the market already priced?

Methodology

One row is one observed value on one date.

Sources. Macroeconomic series come from FRED, which republishes data from the responsible public agencies. Market histories use adjusted close from Yahoo Finance when available. Every series keeps its source link in the dashboard.

Cleaning. Missing and non-numeric observations are dropped. Dates remain at their native daily, weekly, monthly, or quarterly frequency; MacroTrace does not fabricate observations between releases.

Comparability. Indexed views set the first visible observation to 100. Percent change is (last ÷ first − 1) × 100. Year-over-year change compares the latest observation with the nearest observation at least 365 days earlier.

Statistics. Volatility is the annualized standard deviation of percentage changes using 252, 52, 12, or 4 periods by frequency. Correlation uses aligned calendar months and Pearson’s coefficient. Calculations run in the browser from the same bundled snapshot used by every chart.
