Data & methods

Every signal has a source.

Inspect the observations ↗

One panel row is one observed value for one indicator, geography and date. We retain each source’s daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly or annual frequency. Missing and nonnumeric rows are omitted; duplicate dates are not allowed. We do not invent daily readings between economic releases, splice investment histories, or include stock prices.
